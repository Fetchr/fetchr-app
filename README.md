**Fetchr** ([fetchr.fun](https://fetchr.fun)) is a free Windows 10/11 desktop application (clean visual GUI powered by `yt-dlp` and built-in `FFmpeg`) built for downloading videos/VODs in 1080p–4K 60fps and automating stream editing workflows without command-line setup.

## Key Features

- **4K 60fps Video & VOD Downloader**: Download full videos or exact timecode ranges without re-encoding from **YouTube, Twitch, Kick, VK, TikTok, RuTube, Pinterest**, plus audio from **Spotify & SoundCloud**. Built-in `FFmpeg` merges 1080p/1440p/4K video + audio automatically with no daily limits.
- **Hidden / Deleted Twitch VOD Finder**: Recover unlisted or recently deleted Twitch stream M3U8 sources.
- **Chat Overlay Render**: Render **Twitch, Kick, and YouTube** chat with badges and animated **BTTV / FFZ / 7TV** emotes directly onto video or as a transparent overlay.
- **Fetchr Mute (Local AI Profanity Censor)**: Detect and beep or mute profanity and Twitch/YouTube banned words locally on your PC using offline speech recognition (Russian & English) with custom dictionaries and audio preview.
- **Sponsor Blur**: Blur sponsor banners and logos on stream recordings using timeline intervals and automatic region tracking.
- **Vocal Remover**: Separate streamer voice from background DMCA music locally (MDX / Demucs / UVR models).
- **Live Stream Recorder & 24/7 VPS Archive**: Automatically record live streams on PC or connect a VPS for 24/7 stream + chat archiving and instant clip export.
